# Changelog

## [Unreleased] — 0.11

### Added
- **`ScenePlanner` / `Scene` — the resolved-scene core.** A `Scene` is one layout resolved under one `PlanOptions`: style callbacks ran exactly once, label placement is settled, geometry is indexed. `ScenePlanner::new()` retains a growable workspace across plans (steady-state replanning allocates nothing — measured); `ScenePlanner::new_in(&mut [u8])` plans out of a caller slice and reports `E.Render.Plan.026` on a misfit instead of growing. One `plan()` serves both IR backends through a sealed `LayoutSource` trait, and the scene type stays non-generic. The scene borrows its planner and its layout, so a stale pairing is a compile error and exactly one scene per planner is live at a time — enforced by the borrow checker (pinned by a `compile_fail` doctest); there is no `unsafe` anywhere in the planner.

- **Scene element views.** `Scene::nodes()`, `edges()`, `subgraphs()`, `node(id)`, `edge(scene_index)`, and `legend()` yield small `Copy` views, constructed on demand from either IR backend with zero allocation (text, payloads, and waypoints lend; pinned by test) and field-for-field identical across backends. The vocabulary: `NodeView` (with the scene's `NodeKind` real/dummy dichotomy — outer enum exhaustive, provenance inside `Real { origin: NodeOrigin }`; dummies expose `id: None` plus a backend-stable `dummy_of` identity instead of synthetic ids), `EdgeView` (both index conventions named: `scene_index` vs `input_index`; resolved color/weight/markers; storage-neutral `EdgePathView`), `LabelView` with `LabelSlot` (`Inline`/`Legend`/`Omitted` — where a label actually landed), and `SubgraphView` (resolved border/color/label position). Dummies appear in `nodes()` only when the scene was planned with `show_dummy_nodes`, matching paint and hit-testing.

- **`SceneComposer` — the cell answers.** `visit_cells` streams one `CellView` per canvas cell, row-major: the cell's meaning as the public cell vocabulary (`CellKind` with per-arm `ArmWeights` stroke junctions, `CellMarker` marker cells, text chars — decoded views, never packed internals or pre-picked glyphs), its resolved color (the composer is always color-complete, whatever the emission mode), and its hit/pick `owner` — pinned cell-for-cell against `Scene::hit_test` by a permanent gate, along with glyph fidelity against the terminal output. `try_visit_cells` is the early-exit form (`ControlFlow`) for real sinks. The composer retains one workspace chunk sized by `Scene::composition_requirements(&budget)` (checked arithmetic over the exact carve sequence): steady-state repaint allocates nothing (measured), a larger scene grows the heap chunk once, and a fixed workspace (`new_in`) fails preflight with the new byte-unit `GraphError::RenderWorkspaceTooSmall`. Band boundaries are unobservable — workspace size never affects the values.
- **`TerminalRenderer` — retained terminal emission over a `Scene`.** The scene-pipeline shape of the render wrappers: plan once, then `render(&scene, &mut writer)` / `render_into(&scene, &mut [u8])` repeatedly — different emission options, same scene, zero replanning, zero steady-state allocation (a dedicated serial allocation-gate binary proves one scene serving Unicode/ASCII/colored/plain plus a semantic cell visit with zero allocations). It runs the lean terminal profile (no ownership plane; a color plane only for colored emission), and a writer failure surfaces as the new `GraphError::RenderSinkFailed` (`E.Render.Sink.032`) — previously a failing `fmt::Write` could only vanish into a bare `fmt::Error`. The one-step wrappers (`render_string`/`render_with`/`render_to_bytes`) are re-expressed over the same single band-emission path, so renderer and wrapper output are byte-identical for matching options (pinned by test). `Graph::render()` remains a deliberate facade: the cycle banner and simple-chain shortcut stay outside the scene pipeline (now pinned byte-for-byte by snapshots); only the normal layout path goes through plan→compose→emit.
- **The no-alloc sizing story is three exact contracts.** Scene storage: `ir.estimate_scene_size(&PlanOptions)` sizes a `ScenePlanner::new_in` workspace. Composition workspace: `scene.composition_requirements(&budget)` yields `workspace_bytes()` for the semantic `SceneComposer` and `terminal_workspace_bytes(&emit)` for the lean `TerminalRenderer`. Output bytes: `scene.estimate_output_size(&emit)` sizes the caller's buffer. All three are pinned by exact-size tests on both backends (`tests/sizing_contracts.rs`); the one-shot `render_to_bytes` keeps its combined `estimate_render_arena_size`/`estimate_render_output_size` pair, since it plans and composes in a single arena.
- **`CellColor::as_rgb()` / `as_ansi256()` are public**, so non-terminal consumers (SVG, TUI, editors) can read resolved colors — see `examples/svg_export.rs`, a mini SVG exporter driven entirely by scene views. The style vocabulary the views carry (`LineWeight`, `MarkerShape`, `SubgraphBorder`, `LabelPosition`) is re-exported at the crate root.
- **Semantic painter primitives; `charset` removed from `NodePaintCtx`.** Custom node painters draw structure through `NodeRegion::{hrule, vrule, frame, arrow}` — semantic stroke and marker cells, decoded per charset at emission like all engine ink — so one painter is byte-correct under Unicode and ASCII with no charset handling (pinned by golden tests, both backends). Painter strokes merge per-arm with each other and the engine's ink: a rule flush with a frame becomes a tee, a crossing becomes a junction — impossible with raw `char` writes. Text (`set`/`write_str`) still passes through untranslated. The `NodePaintCtx.charset` field is removed; the migration is typically one line (see `examples/node_painting.rs`).

- **Typed, run-scoped diagnostics — and no stderr, ever.** Non-fatal events are now typed data: a `Diagnostic` envelope (stable WDP `code()`, derived `severity()`, semantic `DiagnosticKind`, actionable `hint()`) emitted into a caller-owned `DiagnosticRun` through an object-safe sink (`VecDiagnostics`, `SliceDiagnostics` with a dropped count, `CountingDiagnostics`, `FnDiagnostics`, or the explicit `IgnoreDiagnostics`), finishing into a `Report<T, E, D>` that pairs the outcome with everything collected (the primary `Err` stays authoritative and is never duplicated). `(code, subject)` is the generic identity: `Diagnostic::subject()` normalizes what an event is about (`DiagnosticSubject::{Node, Edge, Configuration}`) so consumers group and dedup without matching every non-exhaustive kind. `Report::diagnostics()` is the unified presentation stream — retained records, then the primary failure projected from the outcome, then its causes (`DiagnosticRef`, via the `ProjectedFailure` trait) — so a presenter iterating it alone misses nothing; `Report::retained_diagnostics()` is the storage view. `SliceDiagnostics` collects into caller-provided `MaybeUninit<Diagnostic>` storage (a `Diagnostic` has no vacuous public constructor to fill an array with; the type is `Copy`, so a buffer is one array literal). Subjects use user-facing identity (node ids, input edge indices). Diagnostics are deterministic across backends (pinned by test). **The `warnings` feature and every stderr write are removed.**
- **Diagnostic-aware entry points.** `graph.layout()` is a fluent builder (`.with_config(...)` then `.compute(&mut cx)` / `.reported()` / `.quiet()`) that delivers the graph's mutation-time diagnostics (implicit placeholder creation, AUTO-involved replacement, clamped configuration) into the run before computing — exactly once: the first run terminal consumes them, later runs report only later mutations, and `quiet()` explicitly discards outstanding events rather than deferring them (no permanent diagnostic history accumulates on the graph); `ScenePlanner::plan(&ir, &options)` is the same fluent shape: a `PlanRun` finished by exactly one of `.compute(&mut cx)` (canonical) / `.reported()` / `.quiet()` — nothing runs until the terminal, and that shape is FROZEN. The existing one-shot render entry points remain documented diagnostics-discarding conveniences. A diagnostic run survives successful and failed planning alike (pinned by test).
- **`add_edge` returns an `EdgeInsertion` receipt** — always, uniform in shape: the new edge's input index plus whether each endpoint was auto-created. Deliberately not `#[must_use]`; statement-position calls compile unchanged. `MissingNodePolicy` (`set_missing_node_policy`) makes the auto-create behavior declared intent: under the implicit default, placeholder creation additionally emits `W.Graph.Node.021` into the diagnostics channel; an explicitly declared policy keeps the receipt as the sole record. A rejecting policy is an additive future variant.
- **`errors::Diagnostic` is renamed to `ErrorChain`** (`caused_by_chain`): it is the error-plus-cause chain, and the `Diagnostic` name now belongs to the diagnostics channel's event record.
- **Self-loops are preserved as records.** A self-loop (`A → A`) is no longer reduced to a marker flag on its node: both IRs carry a parallel record list beside the routed edge list (routing never sees it), preserving the loop's identity (original insertion index), its owning node — by id AND by node-table position, so every consumer joins record→node in O(1) instead of scanning by id (many-looped graphs stay linear) — its label, and — new — resolved style. `Scene::edges()` now includes self-loops after the routed list, as full `EdgeView`s with `path: EdgePathView::SelfLoop { at }`; **style callbacks now run for self-loops** (they never did before — a documented behavior addition); and **self-loop labels reach the legend** (`Gate → Gate: "retry"`) or the diagnostics channel instead of vanishing, closing the gap the 0.10.2 changelog tracked. Routed-list palette indices never shift — loop defaults continue the sequence at their scene positions, pinned by test. Self-loop legend lines are part of `estimate_output_size` (a loop-only graph renders from an exactly-estimated buffer — pinned on both backends). Hand-built arena IRs: `LayoutIRArenaBuilder::new_with_subgraphs` gains a `max_self_loops` parameter, `add_self_loop` takes the node's table index, and `estimate_layout_arena_size*` results grow by one record per edge bound.
- **Clicking the `↺` selects the self-loop.** The loop marker cell now hit-tests as `HitResult::Edge(scene_index)` of the preserved loop — in `Scene::hit_test` and `CellView.owner` alike — instead of the node (the 0.10 attribution, which survives only for hand-built markers that carry no record).

### Changed
- **The crate-root `NodeKind` is now the scene dichotomy.** `ascii_dag::NodeKind` names the new scene enum (`Real { origin } | Dummy`); the IR's flat `Explicit | Implicit | Dummy` enum remains unchanged at `ascii_dag::ir::NodeKind` (its root re-export is removed; the IR vocabulary is revisited in the 0.12 IR overhaul).
- **`hit_test` moved onto `Scene`; `RenderPlan` is no longer public.** The 0.10 introspection surface (`ir.render_plan(&options)` / `ir.render_plan_in(...)` / `ir.hit_test(&plan, x, y)`) is replaced by `planner.plan(&ir, &options.plan).quiet()` + `scene.hit_test(x, y)` — the scene carries its layout, so hit-testing can no longer be paired with the wrong one. `HitResult` gains a `Dummy { edge, level }` variant: a shown routing waypoint reports its SEMANTIC identity — the owning edge's input index plus the level, the same pair `NodeView::dummy_of` carries — instead of leaking a synthetic, backend-specific node id as `Node(id)` (0.10 behavior). All other variants and semantics are unchanged.
- **Render options sorted into three homes.** `RenderOptions` keeps its name and its four `const` presets, but is now a composite of `plan: PlanOptions` (everything that affects resolved semantics: palette, style callbacks, label policy, dummy visibility), `emit: EmitOptions` (charset, color mode, whether the legend block prints), and `compose: ComposeBudget` (band memory cap). Field access migrates one-for-one: `opts.charset` → `opts.emit.charset`, `opts.color_mode` → `opts.emit.color_mode`, `opts.legend` → see below, `opts.palette`/`opts.*_style_fn`/`opts.show_dummy_nodes` → under `opts.plan`, `opts.band_rows_cap` → `opts.compose.band_rows_cap`. The new structs are `#[non_exhaustive]` with `Default` and complete `with_*` builders.
- **The label row-veto is an explicit policy, not a color side effect.** Through 0.10, enabling color AND the legend together silently switched edge-label placement to avoid rows containing nodes. That pair is now `LabelPolicy { placement: Geometric | AvoidNodeRows, overflow: Omit | Legend }` on `PlanOptions`, plus `EmitOptions::render_legend` for whether the legend block prints. The presets map the 0.10 looks exactly: `plain()`/`ascii()` = `Geometric` + `Omit`, legend off; `colored()`/`ascii_colored()` = `AvoidNodeRows` + `Legend`, legend on. The 0.10 "colored with `legend = false`" look is now spelled `colored(p)` + `label_policy = LabelPolicy::default()` + `render_legend = false` (byte-identical, pinned by the migration corpus). Every combination is now freely expressible — including colored output with geometric labels, and a plain-mode legend.
- **Band partitioning is a composition decision, not plan state.** `RenderPlan` no longer stores a band list built from `band_rows_cap`; it stores the level-top geometry, and the level-aligned partition is computed per render from `ComposeBudget`. One plan now serves every band budget (output was already band-invariant — pinned by test). `RenderPlan::band_count()` is removed — band count is a property of a composition budget, not of a plan. `estimate_render_arena_size` results shrink slightly (the stored band list's term is gone).
- **JSON schema `"1.3"` → `"1.4"`**: a `self_loops[]` array (omitted when empty) carries the preserved records — `node` (user id), `edge` (input index; self-loops count in that numbering), and optional `label`. Self-loops never enter the routed `edges[]` list, so their input identity, label, and multiplicity previously vanished from the interchange format; heap and arena serializers emit identical records (pinned by test), and `estimate_json_size` covers them.
- **Plans always resolve colors.** Color resolution no longer depends on the emission mode: a plan built with any options carries resolved edge/label colors, and plain emission simply ignores them. Plain output is byte-identical; this is the groundwork for one scene serving colored and plain emission without replanning.

### Removed
- **The 0.9.x render entry points** announced for removal in 0.10.0 are gone. Heap (`LayoutIR`): `render_scanline`, `render_scanline_to`, `render_scanline_with_buffer`, `render_scanline_to_bytes`, `render_scanline_colored`, `render_scanline_colored_to`, `render_scanline_colored_with_legend` → `render_string` / `render_with` (to match the no-legend colored wrappers: `colored(p)` + `plan.label_policy = LabelPolicy::default()` + `emit.render_legend = false`). Arena (`LayoutIRArena`): `render_to_buffer`, `render_to_buffer_colored`, `render_to_buffer_colored_with_legend`, `estimate_render_size`, `compute_edge_colors` → `render_to_bytes` + `estimate_render_arena_size`/`estimate_render_output_size`. The live `CsrGraph::render_to_buffer`/`estimate_render_size` debug helpers are unrelated and remain.
- **`Graph::add_node_with_size` and `Graph::add_node_with_width`** → declare content that knows its area: `CustomNode { label, width, height, painter, payload }`. For byte-identical 0.10 output, use the `legacy_sized_look` painter recipe from `tests/migration_corpus.rs`; `painter: None` reserves the area without painting it.
- **`LayoutIR::y_index`, `LayoutIR::items_at_line`, and `LineOccupancy`** → `ScenePlanner::new().plan(&ir, &options.plan).quiet()` + `scene.hit_test(x, y)`.
- **The raw color-escape helpers** `colors::get`, `escape::fg256`, `escape::write_fg256` → index `Palette::colors()` directly; the engine emits escapes itself under `RenderOptions::colored`. (`colors::get_custom` and `escape::RESET` remain.)
- **The `SugiyamaConfig` public surface**: the crate-root re-export, `Graph::compute_layout_with` (deprecated since 0.9.0), and — **without a deprecation cycle, as announced in 0.10.3** — `Graph::set_sugiyama_config` and `Graph::with_sugiyama_config`. Configure layout through `LayoutConfig` and `compute_layout_with_config`. The type survives as private storage; `set_crossing_reduction_passes` / `set_crossing_pipeline` keep working.

### Internal
- Every removal was frozen first: `tests/migration_corpus.rs` pinned each legacy API's output against goldens or its documented replacement on 0.10.3 before deletion, and the canonical-vs-golden assertions remain as the permanent migration proof.

## [0.10.3] - 2026-08-25

### Fixed
- **Cluster compaction can no longer overlap nodes**: `compact_subgraphs`' leftward cascade clamps at the leading margin (or the `saturating_sub` floor) without revisiting the nodes it already placed to its right, leaving a level's gaps compressed below their minimum. Members with edges get re-spread by the next refinement round, but a cluster member with **no edges** is skipped by every reclaim pass — so over the three compaction rounds the compression compounded until members overlapped outright (three one-char nodes in one cluster rendered as `[x]x]═[x]`, with one node parked under the box border). Both layout backends now run a last-resort repair after the final x pass: sweep each level in x order and push a node right only when it overlaps its predecessor, restoring that pair's gap rule and the cluster's leading pad for the level's leftmost member. Layouts with neither a node overlap nor a leading-pad violation pass through unchanged — routing quality, canvas, and every pinned output hold, verified by the full parity and quality suites. (A pad-violating layout is repaired even when its nodes merely touch the border rather than each other, so such graphs can render taller or wider than 0.10.2 did.) The proof is retained in-tree: the 4,104-configuration sweep of disconnected-cluster shapes the fix was validated against (454 configurations overlapped before it; zero after) is a permanent regression test, the disconnected fixtures joined the parity mirror corpus, a corpus-wide no-overlap gate runs every fixture through all four directions in both backends, and a 1,025-node single-level fixture pins the repair past the scratch-sort bound the CSR twin originally inherited (repair has no level-size cap — it is a correctness pass, not a cosmetic one). Regression tests pin the invariant in TB, BT, LR, and RL through both heap and arena/CSR layouts.

### Upcoming removals (0.11)
- **`Graph::set_sugiyama_config` and `Graph::with_sugiyama_config` will be removed in 0.11** together with the `SugiyamaConfig` type they take (deprecated since 0.9.0) and `Graph::compute_layout_with`. Configure layout through `LayoutConfig` and `compute_layout_with_config` instead. The two setters carry no `#[deprecated]` attribute — adding one in a patch release would break `-D warnings` builds — so this notice and their rustdoc are the warning period.

## [0.10.2] - 2026-08-10

Label-placement quality and correctness patch. **One thing to check before upgrading:** rendered output changes for any graph with edge labels — labels now place inline wherever they fit, and one shipped rendering bug (a label erasing a self-loop's `↺`) is fixed — so if you pin our output in your own golden tests, they will need regenerating. Unlike 0.10.1 there is **no estimate change**: `estimate_layout_arena_size` and `estimate_render_arena_size` return the same values as 0.10.1, and embedded buffer sizes carry over as-is.

### Fixed
- **Edge labels can no longer erase self-loop markers**: the `↺` is a self-loop's entire visible ink — one cell — and label text always beats markers on the canvas (so labels are never hole-punched), which meant a label window covering that cell silently deleted a whole edge from the drawing. Through 0.10.1 this actually shipped: the hero graph in `LeftRight` rendered `Gateway` without its `↺` because the `"trace"` label sat on the marker cell. The marker cell now blocks label placement in the shared base check every host uses — seeded, slid, floated, midpoint — on both flow axes, pinned by a corpus-wide sweep (every fixture × all four directions × plain and colored must show every marker at its IR cell) plus a mechanical guard test.

### Added
- **`W.Render.Label.031` — invisible-label warning** (opt-in `warnings` feature): when an edge label finds no inline position and the legend is off (the default), the label appears nowhere in the output — previously with no trace. The warning names the edge (index and endpoint ids — label text is caller data and never written to stderr) and the fix (`RenderOptions.legend`). Emitted per plan build; with the legend enabled it stays silent. Known gap: self-loop labels never enter label placement at all today, so they neither warn nor appear in the legend — deferred to a future release alongside their placement host.

### Improved
- **Edge labels place where they fit**: when a label's seeded spot is blocked, the renderer now slides it along the edge's own ink — flow runs and cross segments alike, with three lateral window offsets — instead of silently dropping it to the (default-off) legend. Over the label corpus, inline placement rises from 36/92 to **70/92**; the hero graph places **8 of 8** labels in `TopDown` (was 4) and 6 of 8 in `LeftRight` (was 4; the remaining two are geometrically unplaceable and stay in the legend). Slid placements are held to a stricter collision rule than seeded ones — node bodies on both axes, other edges' ink except shared-trunk cells, markers, and box label rows all block — and placements mirror exactly across directions (for `TB`↔`BT`, exact over the labels both directions place — box-label text anchors to the visual top by design, so feasibility beside it is direction-canonical). Two placement rules landed with this: **no label ever covers its own bend or its own arrowheads** (corner and endpoint-marker cells are excluded in every host), and **long edges label mid-run** — when an edge's longest straight run is at least 3× the label, the label sits at that run's midpoint, the classic wire-label look (`┈┈"feedback"┈┈`), instead of crowding the source or a corner. On the colored-with-legend surface, which keeps the legacy veto against label rows that contain nodes, 64/92 place inline (the hero graph sideways drops to 4 of 8 there — the veto is expensive for horizontal layouts, where labels share rows with nodes by design). Placement work is budgeted like the lane pass — a pathological graph whose every edge carries an unplaceable label degrades gracefully toward the legend instead of paying quadratic scans — and placement queries run against a per-plan index of the edge ink rather than re-walking every path per candidate (an all-labeled ~900-edge worst case plans in ~11 ms; realistic graphs are sub-millisecond). The index engages only on heap-backed plans past a labeled-edges × edges threshold; plans built in a caller-provided arena (the no-alloc/embedded surface) always use the direct query path — so `estimate_render_arena_size` carries no index term at all and arena estimates are unchanged from 0.10.1. Either path gives identical placements, pinned by an indexed-vs-scanned oracle test. Routing is untouched: crossings, bends, overshoot, and canvas are byte-identical.

### Internal
- Both pipelines fuzzed for one hour each at release (~567k executions total; the arena harness lays out and renders through exactly estimate-sized arenas): zero crashes, OOMs, or timeouts.
- New parity gates from this cycle: an indexed-vs-scanned blocker oracle (>10k agreement comparisons), a corpus-wide self-loop-marker render sweep, per-fixture and aggregate label floors on both render surfaces, exact label-plan mirror assertions by edge index, and a single label-visibility predicate (`LabelPlan::paints`) pinned by truth table and shared by the compositor, hit-testing, legend, and diagnostics.

## [0.10.1] - 2026-08-07

Layout-quality and correctness patch. **Two things to check before upgrading:** rendered output changes for any graph with skip-level
edges (long edges now route in clean lanes — if you pin our output in your own golden tests, they will need regenerating), and `estimate_layout_arena_size` returns larger values for such graphs — if you hardcoded embedded buffer sizes from printed 0.10.0 estimates, re-run the estimate; an undersized arena fails cleanly with an error, never silently.

### Improved
- **Skip-level edges route in clean lanes** (both layout backends, byte-identically): a chain-lane allocator gives every multi-level edge a cross-axis lane clear of the *fans* shorter edges sweep — the farther an edge travels, the outer the track it takes, and two long edges no longer weave across each other. Over the routing-quality corpus (15 fixtures × 4 directions): **edge crossings −45%**, bends −7%, worst cross-axis overshoot −66%, total canvas area −0.5%. The hero graph in `LeftRight` drops from 5 crossings to 1 and gets 4 columns narrower. Golden snapshots are regenerated accordingly. The pass runs under a fixed work budget — stress-scale graphs (>16k edges or dummies) keep their previous routing unchanged — and `estimate_layout_arena_size` now includes the allocator's scratch for graphs with skip edges (zero for graphs without them).
- **`estimate_layout_arena_size` is exact for cyclic graphs**: the estimator now mirrors the layout's own cycle breaking instead of an unflipped relaxation that undercounted dummies on broken cycles (an ordered N-cycle estimated zero waypoints where the layout creates N−2 — overflowing waypoints were then silently dropped from the routing). Exactly estimate-sized arenas are pinned on ordered cycles at N=8, 60 and 16,386 with `include_dummy_nodes`, asserting every waypoint is emitted.
- **Layout is deterministic across processes**: cluster block ordering no longer inherits `HashMap` iteration order (seeded per process), which could lay the same clustered graph out differently from one run to the next. Pinned by a regression test with eight average-tied blocks (~1/40,320 chance of a false pass).

### Internal
- Both pipelines fuzzed for one hour each under coverage-guided libFuzzer (~889k executions total; the arena harness generates cycles and lays out with exactly estimate-sized arenas): zero crashes, OOMs, or timeouts.
- New layout-quality gates in the parity suite: an exact LR↔RL reflection check over the full corpus including waypoints, a longest-chain-outermost pin, and a non-regression floor on the corpus quality totals (crossings / bends / overshoot / canvas budget).
- The clustered stress tiers moved to `examples/shared/stress_graphs.rs`, shared between `subgraph_stress` and the quality scorer.

## [0.10.0] - 2026-08-02

The largest release so far. In short:

- **All four rank directions**, with `LeftRight`/`RightLeft` laid out natively rather than transposed.
- **One render engine** for both pipelines, replacing three separate renderers — streaming, banded, no-alloc capable, with introspection and hit-testing.
- **Nodes are objects**: `add_node` takes any `impl NodeContent`, and a node's declaration drives its size and how it paints.
- **Per-element styling** through plain `fn` callbacks resolved once at plan time.
- **Charsets**: `Ascii` alongside `Unicode`, as equal projections of one semantic canvas.
- Substantial layout fixes, most of them **cross-backend divergences** the new parity suite caught.

Upgrading from 0.9.x: see [docs/migrate-from-0.9.md](docs/migrate-from-0.9.md).
Read *Breaking Changes* and *Known limitations and trade-offs* before you do.

### Added
- **Dummy-node visualization (`LayoutConfig.include_dummy_nodes`)**: the flag existed but was never honored; it now works. When enabled, skip-level routing dummies appear in both IRs as nodes with `kind == NodeKind::Dummy`, a new `edge_index` back-link to their owning edge (zigraph parity; `None`/`usize::MAX` for real nodes), synthetic ids excluded from `node_by_id`, width 1 at the drawn waypoint column. Zero cost when disabled (default). The JSON `edge_index` field — previously always `null` — now carries the value.
- **Rank direction (`Direction`) — all four directions**: `graph.set_direction(...)` / `LayoutConfig.direction` select the rank direction; parses from `"TB"`/`"TD"`/`"BT"`/`"LR"`/`"RL"`; re-exported from the crate root. `TopDown`/`BottomUp` stack levels as rows. **`LeftRight`/`RightLeft` are laid out natively**: levels become COLUMNS sized by node widths, in-level order stacks vertically by node heights, and edges route through horizontal trunks entering and leaving nodes at their side faces — a wide, shallow graph reads far better sideways than a transposed vertical one would (text cells are asymmetric, so a transpose is not possible). One pipeline serves every direction: it computes in role space (level axis / cross axis) with a zero-sized, monomorphized axis profile naming which physical axis each role is, and coordinates materialize at IR emission. The mirrored directions are flips of the finished layout — `BottomUp` on y, `RightLeft` on x — so the IR always matches rendered cells, in every direction. Both layout backends and the render engine support all four; the LR/RL corpus is pinned byte-identical heap↔CSR, plain and colored, at every band cap.
- **Unified render engine (`render::engine`)**: one paint path for both IRs — the render layer has no "backends". Public surface on both `LayoutIR` and `LayoutIRArena`: `render_with(&RenderOptions, &mut impl fmt::Write)` (streaming-primary), `render_string`, and a zero-allocation `render_to_bytes(&RenderOptions, &Arena, &mut [u8])` with `estimate_render_arena_size`/`estimate_render_output_size`; `render_plan`/`render_plan_in` expose the introspectable `RenderPlan` (dimensions, band count, legend entries, `hit_test`); `RenderOptions` selects color mode (`None`/`Ansi256`/`TrueColor`), charset (`Unicode`/`Ascii` — equal projections of one semantic canvas), legend, dummy-marker visibility, and `band_rows_cap` (default 64): rendering is banded, so canvas memory is `width × min(band_rows_cap, height)` cells regardless of graph height, and overlapping horizontal spans are painted as merged runs (Massive Fan 50k render: 17 s → 0.08 s). Undersized caller buffers report WDP `Render` codes (`E.Render.Plan.026`, `E.Render.Canvas.026`, `E.Render.Sink.026`) with actionable hints. The legacy entry points survive as deprecated engine-backed wrappers (see *Deprecated*).
- **Per-element styling (render engine)**: `RenderOptions` carries three plain-`fn` style callbacks resolved once per element at plan time — never per cell. `EdgeStyle` (color, `LineWeight` light/dashed/double, `MarkerShape` at either end — suppress arrowheads or make edges double-headed), `SubgraphStyle` (`SubgraphBorder` double/light/dashed/none, border color, `LabelPosition` inside-top/bottom), `EdgeLabelStyle` (color). Defaults reproduce the legacy output byte-for-byte. `const` presets in one place: `plain`, `colored`, `ascii`, `ascii_colored` (the colored legend's "from → to" arrow follows the charset: `->` in ASCII).

- **Typed node handles + `AUTO` numbering**: `add_node` (and `add_node_with_size`, whose id slot stays explicit-`usize` — it does not grow the `AUTO` capability, being superseded by content objects that know their size) now return a `NodeId` handle, and the edge and subgraph-placement APIs accept `impl Into<NodeId>` — so graphs build end-to-end without hand-tracked integer ids. Handles *reduce* the typo'd-id-creates-a-phantom-node trap the `warnings` feature exists to flag; they do not close it — raw `usize` ids (including bare integer literals) remain accepted everywhere, and a `NodeId` carries no graph provenance, so a handle from one graph is accepted by another. `add_node`'s id slot takes an explicit id **or** the new `AUTO` sentinel (`g.add_node(AUTO, "label")`), which assigns the next id above every id the graph has seen: assignments remain collision-free until the counter saturates at `usize::MAX` (only reachable by explicitly parking ids there); subsequent duplicates use the existing replacement semantics and, like any AUTO-involved replacement, emit a `W.Graph.Node.007` diagnostic under the `warnings` feature. `AUTO` is not accepted where an existing node is referenced (`add_edge(AUTO, …)` is a compile error). Re-exported from the crate root: `NodeId`, `AUTO`, `Auto`, `IdOrAuto`.
- **Nodes as objects (`NodeContent`)**: `add_node`'s content slot accepts any `impl NodeContent` — a bare `&str` (the classic `[label]` node, byte-identical to every previous release), the built-ins `SimpleNode`/`BoxedNode` (a boxed node reserves `label+4 × 3`), `&T` references (content objects can be reused across graphs), or user types / the explicit `CustomNode { label, width, height, painter, payload }` form — size, painter, and payload all belong to the object; there is deliberately no `.sized` adapter and no style-side override. Objects resolve **once at insertion** into plain data — label, size, kind tag, optional painter `fn`, payload — so the object may be a temporary and no trait objects are stored anywhere. The declared kind travels both IRs (`LayoutNode`/`LayoutNodeArena.content_tag`; the CSR pipeline packs it into the existing per-node flags word, and carries painters + payloads through the arena pipeline) **and drives rendering directly**: boxed declarations render boxes, declared painters run with their declared payload, byte-identically on both backends — including graphs built directly on the no-alloc `CsrGraphBuilder` (the embedded front door accepts the same content vocabulary and is pinned by a direct-built-vs-converted parity test). JSON export carries the declaration: every node gains `"content_kind": "simple" | "boxed" | "custom"` (a separate field from the layout `"kind"`), and custom nodes export their `"payload"` string — painters are code and never serialize. Storage is sparse + packed: graphs using only simple/boxed nodes pay one logical tag byte per node plus vector capacity/headers (heap) / 0 extra stride (CSR packs the tag into existing flags), and only nodes with a painter or payload get a side-table entry (~32 B + payload bytes). Re-exported from the crate root: `NodeContent`, `SimpleNode`, `BoxedNode`, `CustomNode`.
- **The legend works in plain mode**: `RenderOptions.legend = true` now lists unplaced edge labels in colorless renders too, as self-keying `from → to: "label"` lines (`->` under the ASCII charset) with no escape bytes. Previously the legend existed only in colored renders; plain labels that failed geometric placement were silently dropped. Defaults are unchanged — `plain()` keeps `legend = false`.
- **Everyday names re-exported from the crate root**: `ascii_dag::{RenderOptions, Palette, Charset, ColorMode}`.
- **WDP-coded warnings that cannot panic**: the crate's diagnostic warnings now carry WDP `W.` codes beside the existing `E.` error codes — `W.Graph.Node.021` (auto-created placeholder), `W.Graph.Node.007` (AUTO-involved replacement, new), `W.Graph.Dag.003` (config value clamped) — and are emitted through one best-effort writer that discards stderr write failures: previously each site called `eprintln!`, which panics when stderr is closed (e.g. a daemonized process), violating the crate's no-panic discipline for a mere diagnostic.
- **Custom node painters (render engine)**: a node's reserved `width × height` area is actually filled at render time, steered **only by the node's declaration** (see *Nodes as objects*): a simple node draws `[label]`, a boxed node a light-stroke box spanning the full area with the label inside (falling back to the simple look below 3 rows or 2 columns), and a custom node runs its declared painter — a plain `fn(&mut NodeRegion, NodePaintCtx)` drawing through a clipped, node-local writer (coordinates are node-relative; out-of-region writes are silent no-ops, so painters cannot touch neighboring cells and need no `unsafe`). A custom node with no painter is **blank**: the area stays reserved (edges route around it), nothing paints — a spacer or overlay hook. `NodePaintCtx` (`#[non_exhaustive]`, engine-created) carries the node's id, label, dimensions, the active charset (painter text passes through untranslated), the declared `payload`, and `visible_rows` — the node-local row range visible in the band being composited, so tall painters can skip clipped content. Hit-testing treats a node's full reserved rectangle as the node. A node spanning multiple bands has its painter replayed per band; drawing the same content each call is the painter's contract (see `NodeRegion` docs — canvas isolation is per logical cell; wide/control characters remain the caller's concern, as in labels). There is deliberately no node style vocabulary at all: what a node is — and how it looks — comes from its declaration alone (edge and subgraph styling are unaffected). See `examples/node_painting.rs`.
- **Per-edge trunk axis (`FlowAxis`)**: `LayoutEdge` and `LayoutEdgeArena` gain `flow_axis: FlowAxis` (`Y` | `X`) — which physical axis the edge's trunk (flow segment) runs along, set by layout as geometry. Every `TopDown`/`BottomUp` edge is `Y`; the field exists so path consumers select their formulas from the edge itself instead of inferring orientation from `Direction` or endpoint differences (ambiguous for corner edges, whose endpoints differ on both axes). Direction flips copy it verbatim.
- **Self-loop marker cell in the IR (`self_loop_at`)**: `LayoutNode.self_loop_at: Option<(usize, usize)>` and `LayoutNodeArena.self_loop_at` (sentinel `(usize::MAX, usize::MAX)`) carry the physical `↺` marker cell, computed by layout — one cell past the node on the cross axis at its level-leading line (for vertical flows: right of the top row, the position the renderer has always used). `has_self_loop` is now **derived** from this field — the two never disagree; the arena builder's `set_self_loop` derives both.

### Improved
- **`Graph::render()` / `render_to()`** (unchanged API) render through the unified engine. The cycle banner is untouched; the simple-chain shortcut now applies only to `TopDown` (see *Fixed*).
- **Vertical compaction**: level bands no longer pay for rows they don't use. The edge-label row is budgeted per level (only where a labeled edge is sourced — previously one labeled edge anywhere cost every level in the graph two extra rows, one of them permanently blank), and skip-level edges claim routing rows only where they actually change column (straight pass-throughs render as plain verticals — previously every intermediate level charged one row per passing edge, jogging or not). Hero example: 50 → 45 rows. Both layout paths, pinned by cross-backend parity tests.
- **Tighter subgraph layouts**: a per-level tightening pass reclaims the horizontal slack left behind when sibling clusters are shifted apart, so children align under their parents again. Both layout paths.
- **Inter-cluster compaction**: root clusters and loose nodes are pulled back together after overlap separation, removing the empty gulfs between boxes (up to −24% canvas width on the stress tiers). Both layout paths.
- **Edges never cross node text**: dummy waypoints are realigned when compaction moves their endpoints and nudged out of node spans; an edge that must cross something crosses a subgraph border (rendered as a junction), never a node. Both layout paths.

### Fixed
- **Nested-cluster layouts could silently diverge between backends** (hero-graph class): the *envelope projection* used by the cluster compaction passes expanded a parent by `SUBGRAPH_H_PAD` (2) around each child in the CSR backend but by the shared parent-gap rule (1) in the heap backend — one column per nesting level, which cascaded through per-level frontiers into different dummy columns, different kept waypoints, a different routing floor, and ultimately different box heights and row counts. Both projections now use `geometry::PARENT_CHILD_H_GAP` (the heap one dropped its local duplicate of the constant), and so does the parent box itself — the CSR path drew it one column too wide per side. The CSR backend also anchors skip-level and side-channel edge labels the way the heap backend always did (first waypoint / channel column instead of the source column). The full-featured hero graph is now pinned byte-identical across backends — plain, colored, and banded — in the parity suite.
- **CSR layout depth is no longer capped at 256 levels**: per-level layout buffers were fixed-size (256), so deeper graphs crashed with an index-out-of-bounds (later a clean `ExceedsMaxLevels`). Buffers are now sized from each graph's real depth — computed before allocation — so depth is bounded only by the index type's node capacity (`arena-idx-u8`: 255; u16: 65,535; u32: ~4B) and the arena the caller provides. Wide, shallow graphs get *cheaper*: the old design charged every graph ~35 KB for 256 levels; a 3-level graph now pays ~0.4 KB, and `estimate_layout_arena_size` computes the exact depth (falling back to node count only for unbroken cycles), so provisioned arenas track the real graph shape. Deep chains are pinned byte-identical across backends at 20,000 levels with exactly estimate-sized arenas. Unbroken cycles (`CycleBreaking::None`) that pump level relaxation past any DAG-possible depth still fail cleanly with `ExceedsMaxLevels`.
- **CSR pipeline lost implicit-node identity**: auto-created nodes (referenced by an edge but never added) rendered as `NodeKind::Explicit` in the arena IR while the heap IR marked them `Implicit` — invisible until a node style consulted `is_implicit`. The CSR conversion now carries the flag (`CsrGraph::node_is_implicit`), and both backends agree.
- **CSR slot allocator now matches the heap allocator's decisions**: the CSR path tracked one bounding box per routing slot where the heap path keeps per-slot interval lists, so the two backends routed fan-ins differently (CSR could not share a routing row between disjoint spans). The CSR allocator now uses pooled interval lists with the identical accept/reject logic — the backends produce byte-identical routing (Massive Diamond CSR output shrank ~20% to exactly the heap output). Costs a temp-arena interval pool (two entries per edge).
- **CSR canvas width double-counted its margins**: the CSR path added the routing buffer and label margin into `max_width` and then again into the canvas width, advertising an IR up to 12 columns wider than the heap backend's for the same graph (invisible in rendered text — lines are right-trimmed — but wrong in `width()` and oversized render buffers). Both backends now agree; caught by the render engine's `LayoutView` equivalence tests.
- **O(E²) two-node-cycle detection in the CSR layout path**: membership is now precomputed in one O(E log E) pass (sort by normalized endpoint pair, scan runs for anti-parallel twins with opposite back flags). Massive Diamond 50k CSR layout+render: 6.2 s → 88 ms (70×). **The heap path also gains the feature it silently lacked**: a 2-node cycle sharing a column now renders its anti-parallel pair side by side (`↓` next to `⇡`) in both backends, byte-identically — previously the heap drew both edges overlapping in one column.
- **O(N·E) self-loop scan in the heap layout path**: `has_self_loop` was computed with a full edge scan per node; now a single O(E) pre-pass. Massive Diamond (50k nodes): 2.57 s → 0.38 s (6.7×); 20k: 3.5×. (The CSR path was already O(E).)
- **Heap/CSR layout parity + readable back-edge arrows**: the two backends disagreed on edge-routing geometry, the CSR path silently dropped edge labels (it placed them on its own corner-routing row, where the renderer's collision check discards them), and a reversed edge's `⇡` arrowhead could be embedded in a horizontal routing run (`──⇡──`). Both backends now share the routing rules in `geometry.rs`: corners start one row below the source, edge labels sit on the first row beneath the level's routing block, and every reversed edge's arrowhead cell is pre-reserved in the slot allocator (*arrow-cell reservation*) so a horizontal span that would cross an arrowhead is pushed to a deeper slot — only levels with a genuine conflict pay an extra row. The heap slot allocator now works in layout direction (back edges participate), matching CSR. Pinned by cross-backend parity tests: IR geometry and byte-identical rendered text.
- **`LayoutConfig` spacing is now honored**: `node_spacing` and `level_spacing` were silently ignored (gaps hardcoded); both now apply in the heap and CSR layout paths. `level_spacing` default changed 2 → 0 to keep existing output unchanged.
- **Cluster-width feedback**: unaffiliated nodes are pushed clear of subgraph borders (label-widened or inherited from wider levels), and the canvas now covers label-widened borders instead of clipping them. A subgraph label's contribution to box width is capped at 40 chars (longer labels are truncated by the renderer). Applies to both layout paths.
- **`Graph::render()` honors the rank direction for simple chains**: under `RenderMode::Auto` a simple chain took a compact left-to-right shortcut whose arrow and traversal are hard-coded, so `set_direction(BottomUp)` (and now `RightLeft`) was silently ignored by the convenience renderer even though `compute_layout()` honored it. `Auto` now uses the shortcut only for `TopDown`; every other direction goes through the layout pipeline. Asking for `RenderMode::Horizontal` explicitly still gives the chain form, whatever the direction.
- **Cluster borders at the canvas edge grow the canvas instead of clipping**: a subgraph bottom border pushed off an edge-routing row at the very bottom of the canvas could previously extend past the canvas height and get clipped; the canvas now covers it (rare corner — a routing row under the last level's closing border). Both layout paths.
- **Self-loop markers survive `node_spacing = 0`**: an interior node's `↺` marker cell was silently painted over by its cross-axis neighbor at zero spacing; the cell is now reserved in the level packing (one extra cell after self-loop nodes, applied **only** at `node_spacing == 0` — layouts with any other spacing are byte-identical to before). Both layout paths, pinned by a cross-backend parity test.

### Deprecated
- **The 0.9.x render entry points** are now thin wrappers over the unified engine, marked `#[deprecated]` and removed in 0.11 — each note names its replacement. Heap: `render_scanline`, `render_scanline_to`, `render_scanline_with_buffer`, `render_scanline_to_bytes`, `render_scanline_colored`, `render_scanline_colored_to`, `render_scanline_colored_with_legend` → `render_string`/`render_with`. Arena: `render_to_buffer`, `render_to_buffer_colored`, `render_to_buffer_colored_with_legend`, `estimate_render_size` → `render_to_bytes` + `estimate_render_arena_size`/`estimate_render_output_size` (the legacy sizing contract still works: `estimate_render_size` now returns engine-adequate sizes, and the engine's working memory is carved from the caller's scratch buffer — still zero allocation). The colored arena wrappers require the `alloc` feature (their legacy signatures provide no safely reusable memory for a no-alloc engine render; no-alloc callers use `render_to_bytes`). Also deprecated: `LayoutIR::y_index`/`items_at_line` and `LineOccupancy` (use `render_plan` + `hit_test`), and the raw escape helpers in `render::colors` (`get`, `escape::fg256`, `escape::write_fg256`).
- **`add_node_with_size` is deprecated** (removed in 0.11): size is a property of the node object now — declare content that knows its area (`CustomNode { label, width, height, painter, payload }` or your own `NodeContent` impl). The deprecated method keeps its plain `usize` id slot (it does not grow the `AUTO` capability).

### Breaking Changes
- **`generic` now requires `std` (was `alloc`)**: the feature has never compiled without it. Its algorithms keep visited sets and id→index maps keyed by *your* id type, bounded `Eq + Hash`; without `std` the crate aliases `HashSet`/`HashMap` to `BTreeSet`/`BTreeMap`, which want `Ord` instead — so `--no-default-features --features generic` failed with 26 trait-bound errors in every release that advertised it. `generic = ["std"]` makes Cargo's metadata match what the code can actually do. Default builds are unaffected (`default = ["std", "generic"]`), and the embedded path — `--no-default-features --features arena` — never involved `generic` at all. A `no_std` build that asks for `generic` now fails at feature resolution with a missing-`std` error rather than a wall of bound errors. Restoring the capability needs a `core`-compatible hash set and map keyed on `Eq + Hash`; it is not otherwise blocked.
- **Legacy renderer implementations deleted** (`render/scanline.rs`, `ir/arena_render.rs`, `ir/arena_colored.rs`, ~1,900 lines): every paint rule now exists exactly once, in the engine. Rendered output for graphs with overlapping edge routes changes per the 0.10.0 rulings — junction cells merge (`├ ┼ ┬`) where the old plain renderer lossily kept the last corner drawn; nested boxes sharing a border cell merge into proper junctions; implicit nodes paint at their IR width; unfittable edge labels are skipped (never truncated mid-word). The hero golden snapshot is regenerated accordingly (6 lines changed, all junction glyphs).
- **`Graph::estimate_layout_arena_size` is now built from the layout's full allocation manifest** — node, edge, *and* subgraph label bytes; subgraph-aware IR sizing; back-edge, per-level, and routing-floor scratch; saturating arithmetic. A new `estimate_layout_arena_size_with(&LayoutConfig)` covers configuration-dependent output (`include_dummy_nodes` grows the IR); the no-argument form assumes `LayoutConfig::standard()`. Exactly estimate-sized arenas are pinned by tests on labeled, clustered, dummy-emitting graphs.
- **`Arena` soundness pass**: `alloc_slice_default` now initializes every element through raw pointers *before* forming the `&mut [T]` — sound for any `T: Copy + Default`, including enums (previously it created a typed slice over uninitialized bytes). `alloc_slice_zeroed`, `alloc_slice_uninit`, `reset`, and `restore_position` are now `unsafe fn`: the first two hand out slices whose validity depends on caller claims (all-zero must be a valid `T`; uninitialized contents must be written before any read), and the rewind operations invalidate memory that still-live slices may reference — obligations the borrow checker cannot enforce. Prefer `alloc_slice_default` (safe) and one-arena-per-render lifetimes.
- **`GraphError` and the render vocabulary enums are now `#[non_exhaustive]`** (`Charset`, `ColorMode`, `LineWeight`, `MarkerShape`, `SubgraphBorder`, `LabelPosition`, `LabelPlacement`, `HitResult`): exhaustive `match`es need a `_` arm once, and future variants stop being breaking changes.
- **Deprecated colored arena wrappers approximate, not replicate**: `render_to_buffer_colored*` render through the engine — caller-supplied `edge_colors` are ignored (the engine assigns the same modulo colors the documented `compute_edge_colors` flow produced) and unrecognized palette slices fall back to `Palette::Ansi`. Undersized work buffers are still rejected with `None`. Callers with custom color assignments should move to `render_to_bytes` with an `edge_style_fn`.
- **Undirected edges no longer paint arrowheads**: the default edge style now honors `LayoutEdge.directed` (all edges built through `Graph` are directed, so typical output is unchanged).
- **Node-construction signatures widened for handles (source-breaking in three corners)**: `add_node`/`add_node_with_size` returning `NodeId` breaks expression-position uses that relied on `()` — e.g. `iter.for_each(|n| g.add_node(n.id, n.label))` needs a `{ …; }` block now; `put_nodes(&[])` on an *empty* literal no longer infers its element type (annotate: `&[] as &[usize]` — non-empty literals and variables are unaffected); and storing `add_edge`/`add_node` as concrete `fn` pointers no longer coerces (they are generic now — wrap in a closure). Statement-position calls and all bare integer literals (pinned by test, including beyond-`i32` values) compile unchanged. The content slot's generic bound loses the old parameter's deref coercions in general: plain `&String` keeps working (a dedicated impl restores it), but `&mut str`, `&mut String`, `&Box<str>`, and other Deref-to-str wrappers now need an explicit reborrow or conversion (`&*x`, `x.as_str()`) — one stress example in this repo needed `&*Box::leak(…)` reborrows. `LayoutNode`/`LayoutNodeArena` literal constructors must add the new `content_tag` field; `LayoutIRArenaBuilder::add_node` takes it as a new final parameter, and `new_with_subgraphs` gained a trailing `max_custom` capacity (size `max_label_bytes` to include custom payload bytes). `CsrGraphBuilder::new`/`new_with_subgraphs` likewise gained a trailing `max_custom` parameter (pass 0 for label-only graphs), and `CsrGraphBuilder::add_node` widened from `&str` to `impl NodeContent` — label-only callers keep compiling with the builder's historical byte-based default width; declared content sizes itself. Use `CsrGraph::required_arena_size_with_content` to size arenas for content-declaring direct builds.
- **`LayoutEdge.label_position: Option<(usize, usize)>` → `label_x: usize, label_y: usize`**: the heap IR now uses the same scalar shape as `LayoutEdgeArena`, zigraph, and the JSON wire format (values are meaningful iff `label` is present; the JSON output is unchanged). Saves 8 bytes per edge.
- **`LayoutNode` / `LayoutNodeArena` gained `edge_index`** (`Option<usize>` / `usize` with `usize::MAX` sentinel): code constructing these structs by literal must add the field. `LayoutIRArenaBuilder::add_node` takes it as a new final parameter.
- **`LayoutNode`/`LayoutNodeArena` gained `self_loop_at` and `LayoutEdge`/`LayoutEdgeArena` gained `flow_axis`** (see *Added*): literal constructors must add the fields (`self_loop_at: None` / the `(usize::MAX, usize::MAX)` sentinel; `flow_axis: FlowAxis::Y` for vertical trunks).
- **`EdgePath`/`EdgePathArena` fields renamed axis-neutrally**: `Corner { horizontal_y }` → `Corner { bend_at }`, `SideChannel { channel_x, start_y, end_y }` → `SideChannel { channel_at, span_start, span_end }`, `MultiSegment { start_y_offset }` → `start_offset`. The old names baked the top-down axis into the vocabulary; which physical axis a level-axis scalar lives on now comes from the edge's `flow_axis` (a row for `Y` trunks, a column for `X` trunks — todays layouts are all `Y`).
- **JSON schema `"1.2"` → `"1.3"`**: the path keys follow the Rust renames (`bend_at`, `channel_at`, `span_start`/`span_end`), every edge gains `"flow_axis": "y" | "x"`, and nodes with a self-loop gain `"self_loop_at": [x, y]`. A v1.2 `horizontal_y` is exactly a v1.3 `bend_at` on a `flow_axis: "y"` edge.

### Known limitations and trade-offs
Measured, reproducible, and not blocking this release — but you should know about them before upgrading. Numbers and method are in [BENCHMARK.md](BENCHMARK.md).

- **Long skip-level edges take staircase routes past clusters.** An edge spanning several levels gets a routing waypoint at each one, and a waypoint cannot be reordered past a subgraph it would otherwise cross — so its path drifts further out level after level, adding bends and occasionally crossing another edge twice. Most visible in `LeftRight`/`RightLeft`, where the cross axis is rows and there is far less slack than in a vertical layout; `tests/golden/hero-lr.txt` shows it. Output stays correct and readable, and total crossing counts are on par with the vertical directions. The mechanism predates this release — the horizontal directions only made it easy to see.
- **The WASM bundle roughly doubled**: 46 → 94 KB with `arena` alone, 95 → 200 KB with default features (both ~+101% gzipped). About half is the render engine's feature set — semantic cells, two charsets, plan and spatial index, banding, color, styling, legend, hit-testing — none of which a caller can currently strip. The other half is layout: all four directions share one pipeline, but both axis profiles are reachable from `compute_layout()`, so the binary carries two monomorphized copies. A feature gate making the horizontal profile strippable is the obvious follow-up.
- **Rendering is slower below roughly 100–250 nodes and faster above it.** The engine trades a higher fixed cost — building a `RenderPlan` — for a much flatter per-node slope. Small graphs pay for that plan; large ones win big (Massive Fan 50k render: 17 s → 0.08 s). The crossover shows in the same place on desktop, Cortex-M0+, and Xtensa, so it is the engine's shape rather than any one target's.
